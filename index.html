About Zhongwen

Zhongwen is a fast, lightweight single-file Mandarin study studio designed for focused daily practice across your core HSK 1, HSK 2, and HSK 3 curriculum without clutter or external backend dependencies.

📚 Curated HSK 1–3 Syllabus

Organizes 434 essential words across HSK 1 (158), HSK 2 (170), and HSK 3 (106) with instant search by Hanzi, Pinyin, or English definition.

✍️ Live Stroke Order & Tracing

Powered by Hanzi Writer to animate proper stroke sequences with radical highlighting and interactive freehand writing quizzes for every character.

🔊 Native Speech & Recall

Uses browser-native Mandarin (`zh-CN`) speech synthesis alongside flip flashcards and bi-directional multiple-choice quizzes.

💾 Local Mastery Tracking

Automatically saves every mastered word in your browser's local storage so you can filter down to unlearned terms anytime.
